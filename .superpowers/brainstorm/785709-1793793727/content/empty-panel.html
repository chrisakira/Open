Which drawing?

A

Heading over nothing

Always on means always on: one rule, one shape, no branch about who is alive. The panel's meaning stays exactly "rows are members", and an empty panel is the honest picture of a squad of nobody. Cheapest thing that keeps the rule you already picked.

B

Hide the panel

No wood, no heading, right edge blank exactly once in the game — when you are dead and alone. Keeps the heading from sitting over a squad of nobody, but "always on" now has an exception, and a panel that can be absent is a panel you have to explain.

C

Heading plus a dash

The panel says the truth plainly: the squad is empty. Reads as designed rather than broken. But there is no locked player-death display — the plaque keeps no skull and the dead player gets no overhead plate at all — so this line would be your death notice before any death UI is designed.
