Which timing?

A

Always on, heading included

One wood panel from frame one, SQUAD over the founder's name forever. Simplest to reason about: the panel is the squad list, and its rows are the members. But a heading that claims a squad while naming one person, and still claims it after they die alone.

B

Waits for the second name

The right edge draws nothing until a join puts a second name in C++; the heading arrives exactly when the group does, and leaves when the last recruit dies. Nothing is ever shown that the state cannot back up. Costs a start-of-game screen where the feature is invisible — and if recruiting breaks, you cannot tell whether it is broken or simply empty.

C

Founder shown, heading earned

The founder's name is on wood from frame one; SQUAD appears when there is a second member and retracts when there is not. Every pixel means a member, and the heading is the one that says "there is more than you". Two states per moment instead of one, so a test has to pin both.

Whichever you pick: no dismiss button, nothing clickable, and a dead member's line closes up. In these drawings the founder stands first and recruits below in join order — that ordering is the question after this one.
