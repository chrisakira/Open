D8 — What happens when the window gets small?

Both cards behave identically at 1280×720 (top strip). They differ at 720×420 (bottom strip): the chat is locked to 42% of the width, and your HUD is a solid plaque, so whatever overlaps wins the pixels underneath it. Actual game supports live-resize; a stretch mode also scales everything a bit. What should happen at the squeeze?

A · Float above the chat when they'd collide (recommended)

Bottom-center until it would overlap the chat, then a single step up above it. Nothing ever hides anything; one breakpoint rule, easy to test, survives even absurd window shapes. Cost: the HUD no longer touches the bottom edge in tiny windows.
